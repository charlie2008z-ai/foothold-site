Free lock screen wallpapers.

Five dark mountains for your iPhone. No email, no sign-up. Press and hold one, then tap Save to Photos.

Set it

  1. Press and hold the wallpaper you like, then tap Save to Photos.
  2. Open Settings, tap Wallpaper, then Add New Wallpaper.
  3. Tap Photos, pick the mountain, and tap Add.

Why mountains

There’s one thing you keep putting off.

Foothold is a calm, private iPhone app for it. You name the summit, promise one small step by a time you choose, and prove it with a photo. Each kept step is a foothold.

A private app: no account, nothing to share or post, and your photos stay on your iPhone. Coming soon to the App Store.
